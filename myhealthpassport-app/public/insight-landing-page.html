Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between the ₹2,499 and ₹2,999 plans?

Both include the full wellbeing assessment with 60+ parameters and home collection. The ₹2,999 plan additionally includes HbA1c (blood sugar) and a Lipid Profile — the two tests parents most often ask to add.

Can we choose home visit or clinic visit?

Yes. You can opt for home sample collection in Hyderabad, or visit our clinic at Panjagutta — whichever is more convenient for you.

What ages is INSIGHT designed for?

INSIGHT is designed for children aged 2 to 17 years, with parameters interpreted appropriately for each age group.

When and how do we get the report?

After collection, results are reviewed by experts and delivered as a clear, colour-coded report with plain-language recommendations across every domain.

Is my child’s health data safe?

Yes. Data is protected with end-to-end encryption and handled per Indian healthcare privacy standards. Parents control what is shared and with whom.
